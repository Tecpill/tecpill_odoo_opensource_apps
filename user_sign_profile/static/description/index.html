Description

User Digital Signature Profile is a powerful enhancement to Odoo's user management system that enables users to add and manage their digital signatures directly from their profile settings. In standard Odoo, users cannot add their signatures to their profile, which means they need to sign documents manually every time. This module solves that problem by providing a dedicated interface for signature management. With this module, users can:

  • Access signature settings directly from "My Profile" dropdown menu
  • Draw and save their digital signature using an intuitive signature pad
  • Add digital initials alongside their full signature
  • Store signatures securely in their user profile
  • Reuse saved signatures across all Odoo documents requiring signing
  • Update or modify their signatures at any time
  • Eliminate the need to sign manually for every document
  • Maintain consistent signature appearance across the organization

This module significantly improves user experience and productivity by streamlining the document signing process. Whether it's approving purchase orders, signing contracts, or authorizing expenses, users can now complete these tasks faster with their pre-saved signatures.

Usage Guide

How to Add Your Digital Signature

Follow these simple steps to add your digital signature:

  1. Click on your user name in the top right corner
  2. Select "My Profile" from the dropdown menu
  3. Navigate to the "Preferences" tab
  4. Scroll down to the "Digital Signatures" section
  5. Click on the signature field to open the signature pad
  6. Draw your signature using your mouse or touchscreen
  7. Click "Save" to store your signature
  8. Optionally, add your digital initials in the same way

Managing Your Signature

Your signature can be easily managed and updated:

  • View your saved signature in your profile at any time
  • Click to redraw or update your signature whenever needed
  • Use the clear button to start over if you make a mistake
  • Your signature is automatically saved to your profile
  • Signatures are available immediately for document signing

Using Your Signature in Documents

Once saved, your signature can be used across Odoo:

  • Signatures are automatically available in document signing workflows
  • No need to redraw your signature for each document
  • Quick signature application for approvals and authorizations
  • Consistent signature appearance across all documents
  • Works with Sign module and other signing features
